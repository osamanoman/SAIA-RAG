User Data Deletion

Your Privacy Matters: You have full control over your personal data. This page explains how to request deletion of your data from SAIA (Saudi AI Law Assistant).

What Data We Store

When you use SAIA through WhatsApp, we store:

  • Phone Number: Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Conversation History: Your recent questions and our responses
  • Usage Metadata: Timestamps and interaction patterns

How to Delete Your Data

Method 1: Stop Using the Service

Simply stop sending messages to SAIA. Your conversation data will be automatically deleted after:

  • Active conversations: Deleted after 24 hours of inactivity
  • Message IDs: Removed from cache after 1 hour

Method 2: Request Immediate Deletion

To request immediate deletion of all your data, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to: privacy@bineyes.com
  2. Include the subject line: "Data Deletion Request - SAIA"
  3. Provide your WhatsApp phone number (with country code)
  4. Optionally, explain your reason for deletion (not required)

Method 3: WhatsApp Message

Send a message to SAIA on WhatsApp with the text:

"DELETE MY DATA" or "REMOVE MY INFORMATION"

Our system will flag your request for manual review and deletion.

⏱️ Deletion Timeline

  • Request Acknowledgment: Within 24 hours
  • Data Deletion: Within 7 business days
  • Confirmation Email: Sent upon completion

What Happens After Deletion

Once your data is deleted:

  • All conversation history is permanently removed
  • Your phone number is removed from our systems
  • You can start using SAIA again anytime by sending a new message
  • Previous conversations cannot be recovered

Data Retention Exceptions

⚠️ Legal Requirements: We may retain certain data if required by law, legal process, or for legitimate business purposes such as:
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Resolving disputes or enforcing agreements
  • Fraud prevention and security

Such data will be retained only for the minimum period required and will be securely stored.

Your Rights

In addition to data deletion, you also have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Object: Object to certain data processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.
